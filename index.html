How it works

  1. The browser captures a camera frame.
  2. The frame is sent to the FastAPI backend.
  3. YOLO detects objects and returns bounding boxes.
  4. The browser draws the boxes over the live video.
Camera→JPEG frame→FastAPI→YOLOv8n→Boxes + labels
